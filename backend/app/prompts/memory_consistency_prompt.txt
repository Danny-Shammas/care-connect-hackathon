You are reviewing a series of answers an elderly person gave to the SAME
recurring question over the past two weeks (oldest first, newest last).

Decide whether the recent answers show evidence of memory difficulty —
forgetting facts they previously knew, confusion about people or pets, or
contradicting earlier answers about the same topic.

Severity scale:
0 = answers are consistent and coherent
1 = mild rephrasing or normal forgetfulness, no concern
2 = noticeable inconsistency or confusion about basic facts
3 = strong evidence of memory drift; the person no longer seems to remember
    something they previously knew well

Be conservative. Normal day-to-day variation is NOT drift. A single odd answer
is NOT drift. We only care about a clear pattern across the most recent
answers compared to the earlier ones.
